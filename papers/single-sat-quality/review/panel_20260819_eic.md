## Minor observations (non-blocking)

### M1. Residual "coverage-ceiling" wording is now inconsistent with the global relabel

Three instances of "coverage-ceiling" survive in the hot-start section, where the term is being *deconstructed* rather than asserted:

- **L1150:** "This constrains the coverage-ceiling claim: proximity to G-BL is initialization-dependent."
- **L1160:** "The coverage ceiling is thus a G-BL initialization effect rather than an artifact of the MOEA framework."
- **L1364:** "The hot-start dependence implies that the coverage-ceiling result reflects the combination of G-BL prior knowledge with MOEA search"

The rest of the manuscript uses "coverage reference." These three are rhetorically referring to the claim being qualified, so they are not wrong, but for terminological consistency consider "coverage-convergence claim/result" or "coverage-reference claim." This is a 3-word find-replace, not a substantive issue.

### M2. §4.4 research-question sentence still says "renders the third objective redundant"

**L627:** "...or whether the θ/ψ_sq geometric coupling renders the third objective redundant in the single-pass, single-satellite setting."

This is the hypothesis being tested, appropriately framed as a question ("directly tests whether..."). The answer is delivered in §6.4 as "indistinguishable under the tested search." It is fine to state the hypothesis in its strong form, but a reader skimming §4 only might miss the later qualification. Optional: append "under the tested search configuration" here too, for parallelism with the conclusion.

### M3. Title undersells the saturation finding

"Balancing Profit, NESZ, and Geometric Resolution" describes the objective set, not the contribution. A title signaling the scale-dependent saturation result (e.g., "...: A Scale-Dependent Saturation Analysis") would better prepare the reader. This is the EIC's prerogative to suggest, not a requirement — the current title is accurate and acceptable.

### M4. Abstract is dense with qualifiers but still within word budget

The final abstract sentence (L57-62) carries three stacked qualifiers ("empirical, conditional", "G-BL-seeded", "under the tested single-pass, single-satellite Stripmap configuration"). Each maps to a real caveat, and the sentence is readable. No change required; noting that the abstract is near the compression limit and further revision should add no more qualifiers.
