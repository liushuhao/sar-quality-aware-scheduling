## Part 2 — Categorized Issue List

### Argument Chain Gaps (AC-)

**Issue AC-1: Primary statistical test (Friedman overall + HV pairwise) declared in §5.3 but not reported in §6 Results; first appears in §7.1 Discussion**

**Section:** [§5.3 Evaluation Metrics] · [§6.1 Overall Solver Performance] · [§7.1 Summary of Findings]
**Problem:** §5.3 explicitly commits to a two-stage statistical framework — "Friedman test for overall differences across 5 solvers × 200 scenarios, followed by pairwise Wilcoxon signed-rank comparisons with Bonferroni correction (α = 0.005)" and Cliff's δ for effect sizes. §6.1 (Overall Solver Performance) presents Table solver-profiles (means ± SD), Table hv (HV means), and Figure 1 (HV distribution + pairwise δ), but never in §6 textually reports the Friedman test statistic (χ² = 506.56, p ≈ 10⁻¹⁰⁸), the Wilcoxon HV comparison p-value between MOEA-2 and MOEA-3, or the pooled Cliff's δ = +0.55 for MOEA-2 vs. G-BL on f2. All of these appear for the first time in §7.1 Discussion, several pages downstream.
**Why this matters:** Breaks the standard "declare method → report results → interpret" argument flow. Readers reading §6 end-to-end cannot decide whether solver differences are statistically significant — they see effect sizes in Fig. 1 but lack the overall omnibus test and the corrected p-values. The gap also violates the methodological promise §5 makes to the reader.
**Suggestion:** Add a short paragraph at the end of §6.1 (after the Fig. 1 paragraph) reporting: (i) the Friedman test outcome χ² / p, (ii) the pooled pairwise Cliff's δ for MOEA-2 vs. G-BL on f2 (the Pareto-sufficient quantity reused in §7), and (iii) the HV Wilcoxon outcome for MOEA-2 vs. MOEA-3. Then §7.1 can cross-reference these §6 values instead of introducing them.
> **[Chinese]** 问题：§5.3 明确承诺采用「Friedman 整体检验 + Bonferroni 校正 pairwise Wilcoxon + Cliff's δ」两阶段统计框架，但 §6.1 仅给出均值±标准差表格与 Fig.1 分布图，始终未在正文中报告 Friedman χ²=506.56 (p≈10⁻¹⁰⁸)、MOEA-2 与 MOEA-3 的 HV 比较 p 值、以及 MOEA-2 vs. G-BL 在 f₂ 上的合并 Cliff's δ=+0.55；这些核心统计量首次出现是在几页之后的 §7.1 Discussion 中。为什么重要：破坏了「声明方法 → 报告结果 → 解释含义」的标准论证流。读者读完 §6 时无法判断求解器差异的统计显著性——Fig.1 能看到效应大小但缺整体 omnibus 检验与校正后的 p 值，同时也违背了 §5 对读者的方法学承诺。建议：在 §6.1 Fig.1 段落后追加一小段文字，报告 (i) Friedman 检验 χ²/p、(ii) MOEA-2 vs. G-BL 在 f₂ 上的合并 Cliff's δ（正是 §7 Pareto-sufficient 判据所引用的量）、(iii) MOEA-2 vs. MOEA-3 的 HV Wilcoxon 结果；随后 §7.1 改为交叉引用 §6 已报告的数值。

### Unsupported Claims (UC-)

**Issue UC-1: Envelope correlation r ≈ −0.95 rests on "20 representative scenarios" with no sampling frame disclosed**

**Section:** [§6.4 Mechanism of Pareto Compression — "Envelope versus schedule correlation" paragraph]
**Problem:** The envelope correlation corr(f₂, f₃) = −0.95, computed across "C1-feasible visibility samples (|ψ| ≤ 45°) computed across all visibility windows from 20 representative scenarios, approximately 3.9 × 10⁵ window–time-step pairs at 10 s resolution", is a key number that anchors the geometric-coupling interpretation: it is the baseline against which the solver-selected schedule correlations (−0.69 · · · −0.17) are compared to argue that solvers *actively* push the correlation toward zero via low-squint convergence. However, "20 representative scenarios" provides no sampling frame — are these 5 random draws per density class? The first 5 seeds of each class? Stratified by target distribution (uniform / clustered / mixed)? How is representativeness validated? Without this, the reader cannot assess whether the envelope r = −0.95 is stable or an artifact of a particular 10% subsample.
**Why this matters:** The entire geometric-coupling mechanism (§6.4) contrasts three r values — the null r_null = −0.51 (i.i.d. θ, ψ), the envelope r_envelope ≈ −0.95 (real visibility geometry), and the schedule r_schedule ≈ −0.69 · · · −0.17 (solver output). The direction and magnitude of the "active solver effect" claim depends entirely on r_envelope being a reliable population estimate. If it were unstable under resampling, the partial-correlation-attenuation argument weakens materially.
**Suggestion:** Add one sentence disclosing the sampling rule. Two terse options: (a) if a deterministic rule was used — "20 scenarios selected as the first 5 random seeds within each of the four density classes (S1–S4)"; or (b) if random — "20 scenarios drawn by stratified simple random sampling, 5 per density class, with the seed list reproducible from the companion repository under scripts/envelope_sample.py." If computationally inexpensive, re-computing the coefficient on all 200 scenarios and reporting both values further immunizes the claim.
> **[Chinese]** 问题：§6.4 用来锚定几何耦合机制的关键数值——包络相关系数 corr(f₂,f₃)=−0.95——是基于「20 representative scenarios」≈3.9×10⁵ 个窗时采样点计算的，但论文没有给出任何抽样框架：这 20 个场景是按 S1–S4 每类随机抽 5 个？还是每类取前 5 个种子？是否按照 uniform / clustered / mixed 目标分布进行了分层？代表性如何验证？缺少这些信息时，读者无法判断该系数是总体的稳定估计还是 10% 子样本的偶然结果。为什么重要：§6.4 的几何耦合论证核心是三组相关系数的对比——零假设 r_null=−0.51、真实可见几何的包络 r_envelope≈−0.95、以及求解器输出的调度 r_schedule≈−0.69 至 −0.17。「求解器通过低斜视收敛主动削弱 θ 冲突」的因果推断完全建立在 r_envelope 数值的可靠性之上；若该值在重抽样下不稳定，则「部分相关衰减」的机制性解释将被大幅削弱。建议：补充一句话说明抽样规则。两种简洁的写法：(a) 确定性规则——"20 scenarios selected as 5 per density class (S1–S4) using the 5 smallest random seeds per class"；或 (b) 随机抽样——"20 scenarios drawn by stratified simple random sampling, 5 per density class; seed list and sampling code reproduced in the companion repository." 如果计算代价不高，补充在全 200 场景下计算的系数与 20 场景值的对比，并报告差异幅度，可从根本上免疫该质疑。

**Issue UC-2: S1 ablation variant-D reversal (no-physics beats full-physics on f1* by 22%) is attributed to NSGA-III crowding-distance structure with zero direct supporting evidence**

**Section:** [§6.5 Physical-Objective Ablation — Variant D analysis paragraph (lines 130–149 of _ablation_section.tex)]
**Problem:** Ablation variant D (f₂ = f₃ = 1, constant placeholders) outperforms the full-physics baseline A on f1* at S1 by Δf₁*% = −22.1% (i.e., D selects 14.7 vs. 11.1 tasks, n_sel +32%). The paper provides a structural explanation: baseline A's Pareto front contains "solutions with fewer tasks but higher per-task quality, which can dominate solutions with more tasks but lower per-task quality in the crowding-distance sorting used by NSGA-III's environmental selection. Variant D, with constant f₂ = f₃ = 1, effectively collapses to single-objective profit maximization within the NSGA-III diversity machinery." This is a plausible *explanation* but is presented without evidence. No data is shown for: (a) the n_sel distribution inside A's vs. D's Pareto-approximate sets; (b) crowding-distance ranks of low-n_sel vs. high-n_sel solutions within A; (c) whether D's advantage disappears under a different diversity operator (SMS-EMOA, NSGA-III with a shifted niche, or plain GA-P-BL). §7.3 Limitations explicitly acknowledges, "the NSGA-III crowding-distance explanation for the S1 variant-D reversal awaits verification with alternative crowding operators."
**Why this matters:** The 22% f1* swing is the largest ablation effect in the entire paper, and the direction is counterintuitive (worse objective-function specification → *better* coverage). If the only offered mechanism is text-only structural inference that the paper's own Limitations flag as unverified, the reader cannot tell whether (i) the crowding explanation is correct, (ii) the result is an NSGA-III parameter artifact (population=100, gen=200, p=12), or (iii) the physical objectives genuinely degrade sparse-regime coverage for reasons unrelated to diversity operators. The high-tolerance bar is met because the counterintuitive result demands at least minimal corroboration to not impede understanding.
**Suggestion:** Add one light corroboration in the ablation paragraph — either (i) a one-sentence summary statistic of the Pareto n_sel distribution, e.g. "Median n_sel across A's Pareto set was 11.4 (IQR 9–14) vs. 15.1 (IQR 12–18) for D, confirming that A's environmental selection systematically retains lower-count solutions at the sparse end"; or (ii) reference the search-budget control result already reported in §6.6 but *inside* the variant-D paragraph to strengthen the structural argument by elimination.
> **[Chinese]** 问题：消融实验 S1 区最反直觉的结果是 Variant D（f₂=f₃=1 常数占位符）在 f1* 上反超全物理基线 A 达 22.1%（对应 n_sel 从 11.1 升至 14.7，+32%）。论文给出的结构性解释是：A 的 Pareto 前沿包含「少任务但单任务质量高」的解，在 NSGA-III 的拥挤距离排序中会占优并「挤出」多任务解；而 D 因 f₂、f₃ 均为常数，实际坍缩为带多样性机制的单目标利润最大化。该解释在文字上合理，但没有任何直接证据支撑。论文未展示 (i) A 与 D 的 Pareto 近似集内部 n_sel 的分布对比、(ii) A 中低 n_sel 解与高 n_sel 解的拥挤距离排位、(iii) 替换多样性算子（SMS-EMOA / NSGA-III 不同 niche / 纯 GA-P-BL）后 D 的优势是否消失。§7.3 局限性中也明确承认「the NSGA-III crowding-distance explanation … awaits verification with alternative crowding operators」。为什么重要：22% 的 f1* 波动是全文消融实验中最大的效应量，且方向反直觉——目标函数「变差」反而带来覆盖率上升。如果论文仅提供一个连局限性章节都标记为「待验证」的纯文字推断，读者无法区分三种可能性：(i) 拥挤距离解释成立；(ii) 该结果只是 NSGA-III 参数（种群=100、代数=200、Das-Dennis p=12）的人为 artifact；(iii) 物理目标本身确实在稀疏区通过非多样性相关机制损害覆盖率。该问题已越过「高容忍度」门槛——反直觉的大效应必须有最低限度的佐证才不会阻碍理解。建议：在 §6.5 Variant D 分析段内补充一条轻量佐证。两种方案二选一：(i) 一句话汇总 Pareto 集的任务数分布，例如「Median n_sel across A's Pareto-approximate set was 11.4 (IQR 9–14) vs. 15.1 (IQR 12–18) for D, confirming that A's environmental selection systematically retains lower-count solutions in the sparse regime」；或 (ii) 在该段内提前交叉引用 §6.6 的搜索预算控制结果（翻倍预算仍无法区分 A 与 D），以「排除预算不足→增强结构性解释」的逻辑链来补强当前段落的论证力度。

### Terminology Inconsistencies (TI-)

**Issue TI-1: Ablation variant labels A/D are cited and numerically compared in §6.4, but formally defined only in §6.5 (forward-reference order problem)**

**Section:** [§6.4 Mechanism of Pareto Compression — two places: lines ~913–916 ("for the full-physics MOEA-3 (variant A) it ranges from −0.69 (S1) to −0.17 (S4), and for the no-physics variant D …") and lines ~949–962 ("With all physical objectives removed (variant D, §\ref{sec:ablation}), the schedule-level correlation at S1 is r ≈ −0.44 — closer to zero than under the full-physics variant A (r ≈ −0.69) …")]
**Problem:** The schedule-correlation contrast between "full-physics variant A" and "no-physics variant D" is central to the geometric-coupling mechanism in §6.4 — specifically, the claim that physical objectives *strengthen* the residual f₂–f₃ negative correlation in the sparse regime (A: −0.69 vs. D: −0.44 at S1) whereas structural packing dominates at S4 (A: −0.17 vs. D: −0.18 identical). However, the four ablation variants (A, B, C, D) are formally defined only when §6.5 is included via `\input{sections/_ablation_section.tex}`, which is line 973 of the main file — *after* both variant-A/D comparisons in §6.4. Cross-references like `§\ref{sec:ablation}` technically exist, but a reader reading linearly reaches the A/D labels and their per-task correlation numbers with no on-page definition of what A and D are; the second mention adds the label "(variant D, §\ref{sec:ablation})" only in parentheses.
**Why this matters:** The A–D schedule-correlation contrast is the linchpin quantitative evidence distinguishing the "physical-objective effect" from the "structural packing effect" inside §6.4. When the labels are undefined on first use, the reader must either page forward to §6.5 or accept a deferred definition; either way interrupts the argument flow at the exact point where the geometric-coupling mechanism is being established.
**Suggestion:** Minimal fix at first occurrence of each label. On line ~913 change "(variant A)" to "(variant A, full-physics baseline, defined §\ref{sec:ablation})" and "(variant D)" to "(variant D, no-physics null, defined §\ref{sec:ablation})". The objective function expressions do not need repeating — a 4–6 word gloss of what each label stands for is sufficient to keep the reader inside §6.4 without paging.
