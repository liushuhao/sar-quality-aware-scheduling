#### Points of Agreement

- **[CONSENSUS-4]** N50 is quoted inconsistently: §6.3 prints ≈50 with bootstrap CI [30, 62], §7.2 prints ≈45. The artifact (`n50_logistic.json`) gives 50.03 (CI [30.2, 61.8]); the four scoring seats agree the number must be unified (EIC W5, R1 W6, R2 W4-part, R3 W4), and the DA's m1 corroborates. R2/R3 additionally require the solver composition of the "deficit proportion" to be stated, since G-SM contributes f1* < 0.95 by construction in dense regimes.
- **[CONSENSUS-3]** The §6.2 statement "across all 200 scenarios … Cliff's δ = +0.88, p ≈ 1.5×10⁻³⁴" for G-SM vs G-BL on f3 does not reproduce from the released data (EIC: per-scenario paired recomputation gives δ = 0.99, 199/200, p = 1.46×10⁻³⁴; R2: δ = 0.990, and §4.3's own pointer "+1.0" contradicts +0.88; R1: the artifact's pooled f3 δ = +0.587 and the printed +0.88 matches the per-group S1 value of an undocumented basis). R3 is silent. The synthesizer's own recomputation on `baselines_200.json` confirms EIC and R2: per-scenario paired δ = 0.99; additionally, per-scenario paired MOEA-3-vs-MOEA-2 f3 δ = 1.00 at *all four* density classes (50/50 scenarios per class), so the printed series 0.88/0.56/0.52/0.32 does not reproduce either comparison on a per-scenario basis. **The disagreement between seats about *which* value is correct is itself the diagnosis: no reported δ is reliably traceable to a stated basis.**
- **[CONSENSUS-3]** §6.3 "collapses from 78% (S1) to 6% (S2)" contradicts Table 5 (S2 = 10%) and the data (S1 = 80%, S2 = 10.0%: 5/50 scenarios with f1* < 0.95 on the MOEA-2 progress artifact). EIC, R1, R2 all require harmonization; the S1 basis (which scenario set/threshold produced 78%) must be stated.

#### Points of Disagreement (arbitrated)

**D1 — Severity of the ablation-figure defect (EIC vs R2 on the same figure).** EIC rates it Minor (W6: regenerate from Table-4 data, add twin formats); R2 rates it **Major** (W1, confidence 5, having recomputed D-S1 values from `_progress.json` via `compare_variants()`: Δf1* = −18.04%, f3 = +24.87%). Note the seats cite the figure under different numbers (EIC "Fig. 5", R2 "Fig. 6") — same artifact `fig5_ablation.pdf` (§6.5). *Editor's resolution:* **R2's Major severity prevails.** The figure is the only visual of the ablation's single significant contrast, and its ±4% axes invert the text's message ("no ablation effect exceeds ~4%"). The fix's cost remains a regeneration, so the obligation is unchanged (must_fix) whatever the severity tag; the citation-number discrepancy is recorded for the authors to resolve against the actual compiled numbering.

**D2 — Attribution of the +0.88 δ (EIC vs R1 vs R2 vs DA).** EIC attributes 0.88 to the S1-group MOEA-3-vs-MOEA-2 comparison; R1 reads the artifact's pooled δ as 0.587 (G-SM) and calls 0.88 the S1 per-class value; R2 recomputes 0.99 and flags §4.3's "+1.0" as a third contradictory value; DA reads the 0.88/0.56/0.52/0.32 series as MOEA-3-vs-MOEA-2 at all classes. *Editor's resolution:* the synthesizer recomputed both candidate comparisons per-scenario on the shipped data — G-SM-vs-G-BL f3: δ = 0.99 pooled, 1.00/1.00/0.96/1.00 per class; MOEA-3-vs-MOEA-2 f3: δ = 1.00 at every class. Neither produces the artifact's series, and the only value whose p-value co-occurs with the printed text (p ≈ 1.5×10⁻³⁴ matches the per-scenario Wilcoxon on G-SM-vs-G-BL) supports δ = 0.99. The authors must recompute every reported δ from the final snapshots, state pair + computation basis for each, and correct §6.2 (≈0.99 on the per-scenario paired basis, or 0.587 on the task-pooled basis with the basis stated) and §4.3's pointer. Both EIC W3 and R2 W2 require the verify-gate assert set to be extended to catch these (→ R3, R10).

**D3 — Severity of the squint-envelope realism issue (R2 vs R3).** R2 rates **Major** (W3): the headlined sparse-regime gains exploit |ψ| ≤ 45° while §3.3 concedes real platforms slew "only a few degrees"; at such squints the stripmap scalings (ρ_az = D/2cosψ, NESZ ∝ R³) are more approximate, and §7.2's "G-SM for radiometric priority" presupposes squinted acquisitions are feasible. R3 rates it Minor (W6): a remark or a cheap 5–10° variant suffices. *Editor's resolution:* expertise-first arbitration defers to R2 (SAR-systems domain). Mandatory: anchor the envelope to an explicit platform class or define it as an idealized capability-superset, and condition §7.2's operational paragraph on squint agility (R12). Recommended: the sensitivity run with ψ_max ∈ {10°, 25°} on S1–S2, which both seats described as cheap (S5).

**D4 — The Pareto-sufficiency construct (R1 vs R3).** R1 (Methodology, Major, confidence 4) demonstrates the criterion cannot discriminate: at S1 the per-objective tests fire overwhelmingly (MOEA-2-vs-G-BL f2 δ = 1.0, p = 3.5×10⁻¹⁸; G-BL-vs-MOEA-2 raw f1 p = 1.2×10⁻⁹; MOEA-3-vs-MOEA-2 f3 δ = 0.99, p = 1.8×10⁻¹⁵), but the conjunctive rule never binds because its f1* leg is anchored on G-BL's definitionally-constant 1.00 — making "no solver attains Pareto-sufficiency" an artifact of the rule, with thresholds (p < 0.005, |δ| > 0.33) that bind nowhere in the paper. R3 (Perspective) praises the operationalization as "a good, transferable construct." EIC accepts the paragraph's conclusion without examining the mechanics. *Editor's resolution:* an **existence/operationalization dispute** — R1's mechanics argument is analytically sound and empirically verified at S1; R3's praise attaches to the *idea* of a statistical dominance criterion, which R1 does not object to. The fix (R9) must preserve the per-objective dominance information R3 values: report per-objective significance tests (which fire at S1), state explicitly that the f1* leg is saturated by the G-BL normalization, and either drop the conjunctive "Pareto-sufficiency" verdict or reframe it as a descriptive summary, not a decision rule.

**D5 — Runtime non-reporting (R3 vs EIC).** R3 (Minor) treats wall-clock as a first-class decision variable for the OR/mission-ops audience — data exist in the repo (median ≈20–22 s S1, ≈66–67 s S2, ≈217–220 s S3, ≈262–263 s S4) — and asks for a table plus planning-cycle mapping. EIC explicitly accepts the current choice ("Runtime not reported — acceptable given the claims are about solution quality"). *Editor's resolution:* compatible outcomes — EIC's acceptance is about the *claims' validity* (unaffected), R3's request is about *usable guidance* (§7.2). The add is free (existing `runtime_s` fields) and strengthens exactly the operational section EIC endorses; classify as should_fix (S6).

**D6 — Mean-over-selected-task normalization (DA vs the design's defenders).** DA (M1, m4) argues that comparing mean per-task f2 between schedules of radically different cardinality (random-init 8.2/20 selected at S1; D-control 70 vs 154 tasks at S3) is confounded: selecting fewer tasks trivially raises the mean, so "statistically indistinguishable on f2 → objective-intrinsic convergence" is an artifact of the metric. R2 (S-note) affirms the normalization as "a thoughtful design choice that makes the quality–coverage Pareto trade-off genuine rather than an artifact of selection count." *Editor's resolution:* both scopes are correct — the normalization is a defensible main-experiment design (R2), and the *control* comparisons must expose the coverage axis (f1*, n_sel) alongside f2 (DA); the data exist (`variant_d_random_init/full.json`: f1_mean 0.319 S3, 0.522 S4; n_sel 70.3 vs 117.8). The reporting fix (R17, S10) dissolves the artifact concern without touching the design.

**D7 — HV metric handling (DA vs EIC).** DA (M4, Major) reads "primary quality indicator" (§5.3) then "should not be interpreted as a standalone quality judgment" (§6.4, only after G-SM's 0.2351 HV surfaces) as post-hoc metric selection. EIC (S1) counts the very same disclosure as a strength of the paper's honesty. *Editor's resolution:* **partial.** The "retroactive fabrication" reading is rejected — the point-count confound is a real technical objection, G-SM's HV is reported (not hidden), and the caveat is disclosed in the Table 6 caption; no evidence supports an intent claim. The structural tension is nonetheless real: readers meet HV as the declared primary indicator before the caveat appears. Fix at sentence level: move/duplicate the caveat to §5.3's first introduction of HV (S8).

### Decision Rationale

The manuscript delivers a genuinely useful negative result — a density-dependent saturation boundary for physics-aware multi-objective scheduling — with a control architecture (ablation A–D, perturbation sweep, random-search calibration, hot-start/random-init controls) and a reproducibility posture that all five seats judged above the ASR norm. Every seat verified the *core data*: all headline means reproduce, the ablation p-values are byte-exact against `ablation_summary.csv`, the correlation decomposition (−0.51 null / −0.97 envelope / −0.63→−0.15 schedule) is physically structured, and the hot-start deficits (−0.393/−0.551/−0.684/−0.475) are byte-exact (R1 S3, EIC S2). No seat found evidence of fabrication or selective reporting, and three seats independently praised the paper's honesty. **But the paper's *prose* is not a faithful mirror of its *artifacts***: two passages in the sections that carry contribution (1) invert the direction of the paper's own data (§6.6, §6.4; B1), and a cluster of reported statistics — δ, N50, deficit proportions, HV means, the f1* exceedance, the Fisher-z summary — does not reproduce from the released artifacts (B2), including two self-contradictory values printed in the same manuscript (N50 50-vs-45; δ = +0.88 vs §4.3's +1.0 vs the data's 0.99). Additionally, the ablation figure hides the study's largest effect, the promised search-budget control is unreported while its results sit on disk, the Pareto-sufficiency criterion is vacuous as operationalized, and the abstract's comparator framing requires rebalancing against the paper's own G-SM (B3). Three seats recommend Major, one Minor; the lone Minor (R1) is the seat that verified the most values and explicitly states "the paper should not be returned for a full re-derivation" — every defect is local and repairable from existing artifacts. That is why this is **Major Revision** with a fast-path note: the revised manuscript's required changes are recomputes, rewrites, and one optional subset experiment, not new full batches. A re-review round is nonetheless warranted because the errors sit in the paper's headline statistics, and the revision must demonstrate that a full number-vs-artifact audit (EIC verdict; verify-gate assert extension) has been executed.
