## Contract Paraphrase

D1 methodology_rigor: from an editorial-oversight vantage point, the contract asks whether the study's design, its treatment of data, its statistical and numerical reporting, and the affordances it leaves for a reader to reproduce the work all clear the bar this field applies at peer review. I read this as a demand that the evidence-gathering apparatus be inspectable and repeatable rather than merely asserted, and that the manuscript not ask the editor to take any part of the evidentiary chain on trust. This dimension is owned by the methodology seat, so my part is limited to recognising what a failure here would mean for the paper's standing, not to adjudicating it myself.

D2 domain_accuracy: the contract asks whether the paper's claims are consistent with current evidence in its own field, whether prior work is represented faithfully rather than strawmanned or flattened, and whether domain-specific terminology and reported results are free of factual error. For an editorial reader this is the guard against a manuscript that is internally polished but substantively wrong about the field it claims to advance. Ownership sits with the domain seat; my contribution is to register that a failure of this kind would override any favourable judgement on fit or presentation.

D3 argumentative_coherence: the contract asks whether the central thesis holds together, whether the evidence adduced actually supports the claims erected on it, and whether any fallacy undermines the spine of the argument. Editorially, this is the test of whether the manuscript delivers what its framing promises rather than substituting adjacency for support. The dimension is owned jointly by the devil's-advocate and methodology seats, so I do not plan a score for it; I note only that coherence failures propagate into the fit and contribution judgement I am asked to make.

D4 cross_disciplinary_relevance: the contract asks whether framing, definitions, and implications are reachable by readers from adjacent fields, and whether the manuscript's interdisciplinary claims are actually substantiated rather than gestured at. For a venue whose readership spans several technical communities, this is the difference between a paper that can be entered from more than one direction and one that only its authors can follow. Ownership rests with the perspective seat; my task is to keep the readership question in view without duplicating that judgement.

D5 writing_and_structure: the contract asks whether the manuscript is organised so that its argument can be followed, whether exposition is clear, whether figures and tables carry their informational load, and whether the manuscript observes the venue's conventions. As the seat that owns this dimension, I will judge whether a competent reader can reconstruct the research question, the method, and the result from the text as written, and whether presentation defects are cosmetic or load-bearing. The standard is functional readability and convention compliance, not stylistic taste.

D6 venue_fit_and_contribution: the contract asks whether the manuscript belongs in the configured venue and whether it makes an original, significant contribution that the readership would value. Within this run's configuration, that means judging whether the integration of physical modelling with mission-planning is a substantive contribution rather than an assembly of existing parts, whether headline claims are sized to what the manuscript will eventually evidence, and whether a mission-planning or remote-sensing reader has a stake in the result. This is a mandatory dimension and I own it, so a failure here bears directly on the editorial outcome.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure

dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: Whether a competent reader can reconstruct the research question, method, and result from the manuscript as written, with figures and tables carrying their stated load and venue conventions observed.
what_triggers_block: Organisation or exposition defects severe enough that the central argument cannot be reconstructed from the manuscript as delivered — sections, figures, and tables fail to connect the stated research question to the reported results, or convention breaches leave the submission not evaluable in its current form.
what_triggers_warn: Clarity, layout, or convention shortcomings that a careful reader can work around without losing the argument line, such as isolated opaque passages, captions that under-specify what a figure shows, or recurring convention slips short of unevaluable.

### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution

dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: Scope fit for an interdisciplinary aerospace, remote-sensing, and operations-research readership; whether the integration of physical modelling with mission planning constitutes a substantive original contribution; whether headline claims are proportionate to the eventual evidence; and whether that readership has a real stake in the result.
what_triggers_block: The manuscript falls outside the configured venue's scope, or its central contribution is either not original or not proportionate to its headline claims, so that the intended readership would not be served by publishing it as framed.
what_triggers_warn: Scope fit and contribution are defensible but narrow, partially overlapping with established work, or framed somewhat beyond the evidence the manuscript is set up to deliver.
what_triggers_fatal: The subject matter is out of scope for this venue and the central contribution claim is either derivative by construction or unsupportable with the study design presented, so that no revision short of a different paper could make it a fit.
