## Contract Paraphrase

D1 methodology_rigor — Adversarially, this dimension asks me to test whether the study's design, the way data are handled, the statistical reporting, and the affordances offered for reproduction would survive a hostile referee who assumes the weakest permissible reading of every method statement. My challenge posture is to look for the point at which a stated design choice either forecloses a competing explanation or quietly licenses one, and to ask whether a competent outsider could reproduce the reported quantities from what is disclosed rather than from the authors' goodwill. I do not own this dimension's score; my obligation is to surface the methodological vulnerability in the form of a challenge.

D2 domain_accuracy — Adversarially, this dimension asks whether the claims as stated would be recognized as correct by a domain expert who is actively looking for misrepresentation of prior work, mis-attributed results, or terminology used in a way the field does not use it. My posture is to treat every "prior work does not do X" and every "the correct formulation is Y" as a claim requiring a source, and to test whether the paper's characterization of what exists is the most favorable-to-the-authors reading rather than the accurate one. The strongest counter I can construct here is that the paper's own framing of the state of the art has been simplified just enough to make its gap appear larger than it is.

D3 argumentative_coherence — Adversarially, this dimension asks whether the central thesis actually follows from the presented evidence, whether any link in the chain is assumed rather than shown, and whether a reader holding the opposite view could dismantle the argument from the paper's own material. My posture is to reconstruct the argument as a premise-to-conclusion chain and attack the weakest link: an inference from simulation outcome to mission-level consequence, an attribution of an observed gain to the proposed construction rather than to the search procedure that produced it, a generality claim whose scope assertion is carried by the framing rather than by the evidence, or an alternative explanation that is at least as parsimonious and better fits the reported numbers. Coherence here is not stylistic smoothness; a fluent exposition that hides a non-sequitur is exactly the failure this dimension exists to catch.

D4 cross_disciplinary_relevance — Adversarially, this dimension asks whether a reader from an adjacent field can follow the definitions and framing without the authors' private vocabulary, and whether claims that only make sense inside one discipline are presented as if they were general. My posture is to hunt for interdisciplinary claims that are asserted as implications rather than substantiated by evidence the adjacent field would accept. I do not own this score.

D5 writing_and_structure — Adversarially, this dimension asks whether the organization, exposition, and figure/table apparatus would let a skeptical reader verify claims without re-deriving them, and whether any structural choice suppresses a checkable comparison rather than presenting it. My posture is to look for where clarity failures become evidence failures: a figure that does not show what the caption asserts, a table whose columns cannot be compared, or a section boundary that hides a discontinuity in the argument. I do not own this score.

D6 venue_fit_and_contribution — Adversarially, this dimension asks whether the contribution is what the authors say it is once the framing is stripped, and whether the claimed increment over the existing body of work is original and substantial enough for the readership the manuscript is aimed at. My posture is to ask the "so what" question at maximum severity: if the central result were true, what changes, and is that change the one the paper claims. Note that this panel run supplies no criteria authority, so no venue-specific alignment judgement is constructed here. I do not own this score.

## Scoring Plan

### D3: argumentative_coherence

dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: the load-bearing inferential links — premise-to-conclusion steps, simulation-to-consequence transfers, attribution of reported gains to the proposed construction rather than to the companion search procedure, and the scope carried by generality claims — plus any alternative explanation at least as parsimonious as the authors' that the manuscript leaves unconsidered
what_triggers_block: a central inferential link is asserted rather than demonstrated and the paper's own presented evidence does not supply it, so the stated conclusion does not follow even granting every reported number
what_triggers_warn: the core chain survives but a materially load-bearing link is under-argued, the stated scope of a claim exceeds what the evidence licenses, or a competing explanation is left unaddressed that a hostile reader could raise without new data
what_triggers_fatal: the central thesis is self-contradictory or is contradicted by the manuscript's own reported results, so no amount of re-argument can restore the conclusion and the contribution would have to be restated as a different result
