## 2. New defects found

- **M1 (MAJOR) — internal contradiction on whether wall-clock figures exist.** Location: §7.1 L409 vs §5.3 L282. Quote: L409 "no wall-clock runtime figure is registered for the promoted runs, so the platform is offered as a falsifiable scheduling substrate" vs L282 "The wall-clock cost of one unit at that budget is 5--15~s (single-threaded; median 4.9~s for the 20-task control, 10.0~s for the 50-task and 15.3~s for the 100-task family on the registered workstation)". Why: the same paper asserts no runtime figure exists and then registers three of them; the sweep units ARE the promoted runs, so no reading of "promoted" separates the two sentences. (Both sentences pre-date the final t55 batch, but the contradiction stands in the revised text and is exactly the C3/C4-class two-sentences-opposite defect the panel dinged.) Fix: qualify L409, e.g. "no end-to-end operational runtime figure --- beyond the per-unit search costs of Section 5.3 --- is registered".
- **M2 (MAJOR) — "three registered rules" overstates the registration status of the two new repair arms.** Location: §6.6 L384. Quote: "so three registered rules are reported … (i) the drop-later rule registered with the counterfactual --- … (ii) a priority-aware drop --- …; and (iii) a bounded re-timing --- …". Why: only rule (i) is registered with the counterfactual (ticket-54 artifact); (ii)/(iii) were added 2026-10-24 as the DA2/T1 reanalysis — they appear in `scripts/run_counterfactual_repairs.py` ("Panel T1 reanalysis (2026-10-24) … reprocesses the SAME stored freeze-dwell plans") but in none of the registries (analysis_prereg.json / analysis_prereg_v2.json / promotion_prereg.json / caliber_registry.json have zero repair-rule entries; formula-spec §8.18 covers only T18).
