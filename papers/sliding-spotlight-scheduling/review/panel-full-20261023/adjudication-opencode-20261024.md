## 2. J2 CRITICAL rulings (DA1, DA2)

### DA1 — confounded causal title

*Steel-manning the manuscript.* The title's subject can be read as the mode each task receives, and on that reading the paper buys its claim with a within-family decomposition rather than a three-cell correlation. Table 2 (L286–294) is a per-family measurement, not a cross-family comparison: inside the mixed family it splits the tightening cost into a floor part and a residual, 20.01 of 26.01 pp at s=0.9 and 44.78 of 46.58 pp at s=0.8 (L274), and the residual falls from 6.0 to 1.8 pp — the floor, i.e. the mode assignment, carries the cost. R1C's step is then not an analogy but arithmetic: the coarse tier spans [2.5, 3.0] m (L240), the floor is 2.4669 m, and 0.8 × [2.5, 3.0] = [2.0, 2.4] m lies strictly finer than the floor, so no admissible mode exists at s=0.8 (L323) — an exact consequence, verified in Table 2's R1C bound column reading 0.000. R2F carries no stripmap task and its curve is flat to within 0.27 pp of the as-placed point at every tighter point (L419). The abstract's operative sentence is task-level — "Which mode a task receives decides what a requirement change costs" (L29) — and the paper scopes the whole reading to "requirement sensitivity under this fixed mode assignment". The paper also concedes the covariation in the same section (L274) rather than hiding it, which is the behaviour of a paper whose claim is mechanistic, not comparative.

*Steel-manning the challenge.* The title's subject is "Mode Composition" — a family-level noun — and the only family-level variation of composition in the design is perfectly collinear with task count (20/50/100), window density (145–162/337–391/731–779, L248) and tier mix. There is no crossing cell: no all-STR family at N = 50, no no-STR family at N = 100, so the three-shape narrative (step / plateau / fall) cannot assign shape differences to composition rather than to capacity. R2F's flatness — one of the three shapes — is equally consistent with 50 tasks leaving re-timing headroom, and no per-grid-point C2-tightness measurement separates the two stories. The arm that could have begun to deconfound does not exist, and the paper's own concession at L274 states the confound without resolving it. A causal verb in a title, asserted from three cells that differ simultaneously on every axis, is precisely what a methods referee rejects; and the title is the paper's most-quoted sentence.

*Ruling: **VALIDATED**.* The challenge is correct on the load-bearing point and I verified its two factual anchors directly (L274's concession; L248's family ladder and R4H retirement). The design cannot attribute the between-family shape differences to composition rather than density, so the family-level causal verb outruns the evidence. Two qualifications bound the ruling. First, the defect is narrower than "no causal claim survives": the floor *mechanism* is supported within R3H by a per-family decomposition, and the abstract's task-level sentence is defensible — the re-review's note that T2 must keep the task-level reading and drop only the family-level one is right and should govern execution. Second, the revision path is proportionate and T2(b) (reword + confound at first mention) is sufficient; the composition-matched family is an alternative, not a requirement, and it is the N = 100 no-STR cell, **not** R4H — the letter's and the re-review's "(R4H)" attribution is the one inherited error in this ruling's paper trail (correction ordered in §3). The severity stands where the letter put it: this blocks silent acceptance and does not invalidate any number.

### DA2 — repair-rule-conditioned counterfactual gap

*Steel-manning the manuscript.* The counterfactual's robust quantity is stated before the fragile one, and the fragile one is disclosed in the same sentence that states it: "The transfer constraint is violated in 20–29\% of consecutive pairs (median over seeds; 184 of 926 pairs for the 100-task family, 135 of 458 for the 50-task family), and after a deterministic time-order repair that drops the later task of each violating pair, the replayed schedule lands 18.25 and 30.44 percentage points (family medians) below what the endogenous dwell delivers" (L366). Nothing is concealed: a reader who cares about conditioning has the rule, the pair counts and the two family medians in one place. The arms are internally controlled — "the same solver, the same budget, the same scenario population" (L364) — so the comparison isolates the dwell model, not the search. The gap is also accountable rather than arbitrary: I recomputed it, and 18.4 dropped tasks over 99–100 admitted is 18.4–18.6 pp against an observed 18.25 pp, i.e. the gap *is* the priority mass of tasks the fixed-dwell plan selected but cannot fly. That is a defensible operational definition of what a wrong dwell model costs: the plan is infeasible against real dwells, and the mass it cannot deliver is the loss. The "would ship the plan without knowing" clause (L366) describes the epistemic state of a planner using a library-dwell model, which is true regardless of how sophisticated the downstream repair is — the computed plan is infeasible before any repair runs.

*Steel-manning the challenge.* The two most-read locations state the number unqualified — the abstract's "lands 18--30 points below the endogenous dwell" (L29) and the conclusion's "land 18--30 percentage points short" (L419) — and neither says "after a priority-blind, no-retiming repair". Because the gap equals the dropped-task mass, its magnitude is a property of the repair rule: a priority-aware drop lowers it, and a margin-carrying or re-timing baseline changes the violation set before the repair is even reached. The paper's own 25% pad semantics show that margin-carrying is standard practice in the very literature it cites, so the "zero-margin library dwell" arm is the crudest available baseline. DA2's underlying point — that "classical schedulers lose 18–30 points" is not what was measured, and that the number is an upper-ish estimate of the cost of the classical assumption — is therefore right on conditioning. The remedy is cheap (the artifacts exist; the paper already ran one replay instrument).

*Ruling: **VALIDATED**, with the challenge's implied repricing corrected downward in force.* I verified the conditioning claim and its arithmetic myself (above), so the finding stands: the abstract and conclusion carry a repair-conditioned magnitude as an unconditional one. But the re-review's own remedy arithmetic — "roughly halves" — is too generous to the challenge, and so is DA2's implicit suggestion that the gap would collapse under a fairer repair. With i.i.d. randint(1,10) priorities, a priority-aware drop removes E[min(pair)]/E[p] = 3.85/5.5 ≈ **30%** of the lost mass at the same violation set, giving ≈12.8 pp (R3H) and ≈21.3 pp (R2F) — still double-digit, still the paper's most exportable finding. So the correct disposition is the one the letter already orders (T1: add at least one alternative arm as a reanalysis, lead with the repair-independent 20–29% violation rate, qualify every appearance of 18–30 pp including the abstract), **plus** two execution conditions: (i) do not describe the gap as manufactured or as an artifact of the repair — it is a measured consequence of an infeasible plan under the registered rule, and the alternative-arm result will show it survives at roughly 70% of its magnitude; (ii) soften "would ship the plan without knowing" to an epistemic statement about the dwell model, not a claim about operational practice, which does plan with margins.
