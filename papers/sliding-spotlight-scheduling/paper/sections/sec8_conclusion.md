# 8. Conclusion

This paper put the required resolution into the scheduling constraint set and measured what tightening it costs. On the platform leg, the imaging mode is resolved from the product requirement and its parameters follow the requirement per task, so the delivered resolution equals the requirement at the selected start by construction; the dwell is then an endogenous consequence of the mode, the requirement and the selected start, and the maneuver-coupled feasibility chain prices every schedule in physically meaningful time. The quality index states a platform capability and supplies a reported reading --- a fulfillment ratio on the library convention --- and it carries no selection pressure: coverage is the only objective, and a unit's front is a tie-set on it.

The result is a coverage--demand curve over a frozen nine-point grid, read under a fixed mode assignment. Tightening is where the coverage moves: the 100-drawn / 99--100-admitted family drops 26.0 percentage points from the as-placed requirement to $s=0.9$ and 46.6 points to the tightest feasible point --- of which the stripmap floor accounts for 20.0 and 44.8 points (the floor-exclusion bound of Table 2) --- the all-stripmap control drops 46.0 points at $s=0.9$ and has no feasible individual at all at the tightest point --- an infeasibility record, not a coverage of zero --- and the 50-drawn / 46--50-admitted family holds within 0.27 points of its as-placed coverage at every tighter point. Loosening recovers less: 4.15 percentage points for the 100-task family against its own saturation and 2.36 points for the 50-task family (0.93 paired per seed). The fixed-dwell counterfactual quantifies what the endogenous dwell buys: planned with the library dwell, the dense families violate the transfer constraint in 20--29% of consecutive pairs and, after repair, land 7--30 points short, the span depending on the repair rule (Section 6.6). Two of the three frozen shape criteria fail on the 50-task family, and both failures are reported as this scenario set's answer; the tight-arm penalty of the previous generation is withdrawn rather than quietly carried forward. The anchor already attains the best-found coverage in 258 of 260 units --- which is what makes the response a construction result rather than a search finding.

The claims stay inside those bounds. They are requirement sensitivity under a frozen mode assignment, at one registered budget, with the capacity law stated in convention and without a number. The sensor-layer question of resolution against swath, the joint mode-and-time formulation, the recomputation of the serial bound under this scenario set, and an operational-cost evaluation are future work rather than results. Two continuations follow directly from the counterfactual: onboard replanning against realised dwells, where the fixed-dwell failure mode bites hardest, and constellation-level tier management, where the requirement tiers this paper tightens are priced products.
