# 8. Conclusion

This paper put the order resolution into the scheduling constraint set and measured what tightening it costs. On the platform leg, the imaging mode is resolved from the product order and its parameters follow the order per task, so the delivered resolution equals the order at the selected start by construction; the dwell is then an endogenous consequence of the mode, the order and the selected start, and the maneuver-coupled feasibility chain prices every schedule in physically meaningful time. The quality index states a platform capability and supplies a reported reading --- a fulfillment ratio on the library caliber --- and it carries no selection pressure: coverage is the only objective, and a unit's front is a tie-set on it.

The result is a coverage--demand curve over a frozen nine-point grid, read under a fixed mode assignment. The 100-drawn / 99-admitted family is where the tightening bites, from 0.4827 at the tightest feasible point to 0.9900 at the coarsest, and it pays 4.15 percentage points at the order point against its own saturation. The 50-drawn / 46-admitted family is nearly flat and pays 2.36 points. The single-satellite control is a floor step: it serves the whole priority mass from the order point upwards, reads 0.5398 at the next point down, and has no feasible individual at all at the tightest point --- an infeasibility record, not a coverage of zero. Two of the three frozen shape criteria fail on the 50-task family, and both failures are reported as this world's answer; the tight-arm penalty of the previous world is withdrawn rather than quietly carried forward. Over the grid the tie set serves 477,308 task instances with no violation recorded relative to the evaluator, and the anchor already attains the unit maximum in 258 of 260 units --- which is what makes the response a construction result rather than a search finding.

The claims stay inside those bounds. They are order sensitivity under a frozen mode assignment, at one registered budget, with the capacity law stated in caliber and without a number. The sensor-layer question of resolution against swath, the joint mode-and-time formulation, the recomputation of the serial bound under this scenario world, and an operational-cost evaluation are future work rather than results.
